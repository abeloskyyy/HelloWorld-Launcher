# Launcher Tips File (HelloWorld-Launcher)
# Each non-empty line that does not start with '#' will be shown as a tip during the loading screen.

HelloWorld Launcher allows you to open multiple game instances at the same time without conflicts.
Did you know you can share your installations and modpacks with the community in the Workshop?
In the installation settings, you can allocate more RAM to significantly improve performance.
Use the Social button to check statuses, play with friends, and chat without leaving the launcher.
You can easily switch between your Microsoft accounts from the Accounts dropdown menu.
HelloWorld Launcher is optimized to consume far fewer CPU and RAM resources than other launchers.
In the News section, you can stay up to date with the latest Minecraft news and patches.
Have questions or need help with an issue? Join our Discord community from the social links.
The Workshop lets you install configurations optimized by other users with just a single click.
When creating a new installation, you can choose between Vanilla, Fabric, Forge, NeoForge, and Quilt.
You can customize the icon and folder for each of your installations to stay better organized.
Background progressive mod loading allows you to browse the store without UI freezes.
